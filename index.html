The weekly Nifty strategy I'm running

A hedged short-put structure on Nifty weekly options. Rules only, no live strikes or premiums — those change week to week and aren't posted here.

Leg 1 · Sell

1 lot ATM put

Sell 1 lot of the current week's at-the-money Nifty put. This collects the premium that funds the hedge in leg 2.

Leg 2 · Buy

2 lots OTM put, next week

Buy 2 lots of an out-of-the-money put on next week's expiry, choosing a strike where the combined cost is roughly half the premium collected in leg 1.

Net effect Opens for a net credit — leg 2 costs about half of what leg 1 collects.
Why the hedge is a week out The next-week OTM puts stay cheaper per lot, so 2 lots of protection still costs less than the premium collected.
Review cadence Positions and strikes are reassessed weekly around expiry — this is a running log, not a fixed set-and-forget trade.
